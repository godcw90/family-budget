Your records.

Current data practices for this personal-use Mac app, including its optional Plaid connection.

Local financial records

The app stores accounts, transactions, categories, plans, comments, retained import evidence and activity/correction history in an encrypted local vault. Unlocking uses protected Keychain access with macOS authentication, or the saved recovery key plus Mac authentication. No security measure guarantees protection on a compromised device.

Plaid bank connections

If you connect an account, Plaid and the financial institution handle the bank authorization flow. Family Budget requests consented account details, balances and transaction history for budgeting, categorization, cash-flow tracking and reports. It does not initiate payments and does not ask you to store a bank password or full payment-card credentials in the app.

The personal installation keeps the owner’s Plaid API credentials and long-lived access tokens in separate device-only, user-presence-protected macOS Keychain records. Connection metadata and pending downloads are encrypted locally. Requests go to Plaid over HTTPS. There is no hosted token service or public webhook receiver in this installation. This describes the implemented personal architecture, not a claim of an approved exception to Plaid’s token-handling guidance.

Automatic checks run only while open and unlocked, subject to daily limits and authentication. Downloaded changes wait for review/save. A local pause or discarded download does not disconnect the bank. Use the app’s guarded Disconnect bank flow to request provider removal; uncertain outcomes require reconciliation before assuming removal or billing has stopped.

Backups and exports

You may choose encrypted manual backups or enable monthly encrypted archives in your own folder, including iCloud Drive. The live SQLite database is not placed in a synced folder. Monthly archives run while open/unlocked and verify local recovery before applying managed retention. Verification does not confirm cloud upload. Keep the recovery key separately; no reset service exists. Financial archives contain saved ledger records, not pending bank downloads or device-only bank credentials. Readable JSON/CSV/report exports are outside the app’s encryption.

Retention and deletion

Saved transaction, source and correction history remains locally available for budgeting/recovery. Disconnecting a bank retains that saved history. Account deletion is recoverable and excludes records from active totals; it is not permanent erasure. Optional source archival and backup retention do not erase original downloads, cloud versions, Time Machine copies or unrelated files. Managed older backups are moved to Trash only after a replacement verifies; modified/unreadable and untracked files are retained. Exported archives and their storage permissions remain under your control. Ask the maintainer before sharing or removing sensitive records if you need help.

This information website

This static site has no app login, financial upload, contact form, advertising or added analytics scripts. Do not enter credentials or recovery keys here. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information under its own practices; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Plaid and financial institutions also apply their own terms and privacy practices during connection.

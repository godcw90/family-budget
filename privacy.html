This page describes the app as it operates today and distinguishes the bank connection still being developed.

Current data handling

Family Budget stores the account details and transactions you enter or import, together with categories, plans, comments, source-file evidence and local activity history. The live budget is stored locally in an encrypted vault. Unlocking uses macOS authentication with a protected Keychain key, or the recovery-key path with Mac authentication.

You may export encrypted backups to a location you choose, including your own iCloud Drive. The recovery key must be kept separately. Readable exports are outside the app’s encryption. No security measure guarantees protection on a compromised device.

Bank connections are not yet enabled

The planned integration uses Plaid to obtain consented account balances and transaction history. Before live connections are activated, this page will be updated to describe the actual connection service, token storage, retention, disconnection and deletion controls. The app currently does not connect to a bank or send budget records to Plaid.

This information website

This is a static information site. It has no account login, financial-data upload, contact form, advertising or added analytics scripts. Do not enter bank credentials or recovery keys here. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information under its own privacy practices; if hosted on GitHub Pages, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Records, archives and removal

The local app retains transaction corrections and history. Deleting an account in the app is recoverable and excludes its activity from active totals; it does not permanently erase the retained records. Source-file archival moves original text into a verified encrypted archive chosen by the user. Exported archives remain under the user’s control. Contact the maintainer for questions before sharing or removing sensitive records.

Family Budget

A native Mac app for a household spending plan and account records.

Current status: Manual transactions, CSV imports and optional live Plaid bank connections are implemented for this personal-use app. Connected accounts download updates for explicit review before saving. Institution availability varies.

What the app does

  • Organizes accounts, categories, monthly limits and expected income.
  • Records comments, category splits and reviewed imports with exact-cent accounting.
  • Shows account snapshots, recorded balances, spending reports and retained month-close history.
  • Keeps the live budget in an encrypted local vault; encrypted backups can use a folder you choose, including iCloud Drive.

Bank connections

Plaid supplies consented account information, balances and transaction history for budgeting and reports. The app checks available updates while open and unlocked, with scheduled daily limits and manual review/save. It does not initiate payments or move money. Banking sign-in happens privately through the bank/Plaid connection flow.

Who this is for

This is Chris White’s personal household budgeting project. It is not a public banking service and has no public user registration. Plaid usage charges are separate from the app; no additional hosted service is required by this installation.

Privacy and support

Read the data handling, retention and contact information. Do not send bank passwords, API secrets, recovery keys or financial files by email. No independent security certification or provider architecture exception is claimed.

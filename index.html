Family Budget

A native Mac app for keeping a household spending plan and its account records together.

Current status: The app supports manual transactions and file imports. Live bank connections through Plaid are being prepared and are not yet enabled.

What the app does

  • Organizes accounts, categories, monthly spending limits and expected income.
  • Records transactions, comments and category splits, with CSV import review.
  • Shows recorded account balances, spending reports and planned cash activity.
  • Keeps the live budget local in an encrypted vault, with user-controlled encrypted exports.

Why bank data is requested

The planned Plaid connection will import account balances and transaction history for household budgeting, transaction categorization, cash-flow tracking and spending reports. Family Budget does not initiate payments or move money.

Who this is for

This is Chris White’s personal household budgeting project. It is not a public banking service and does not offer public account registration.

Privacy and support

Read the current privacy practices and contact information. Do not send bank passwords, Plaid secrets, recovery keys or financial records by email.
